Why once removed

Doing the work and judging the work are different jobs.

The selfdriven observer framework: machines watch at volume; people decide. GCP already separates monitoring, quality control and independent audit at the level of the organisation. This brings the same separation down to everyday work.

When you do the workWhen you observe the work
You check what you remember doingYou check what the record shows
Self-review bias: you see what you meant to writeNo authorship to defend
You sample because time is short: 5 of 38 consent formsObservers read 38 of 38; you review only what they raise
The report is a narrative you wroteThe report is a signed record anyone can verify
Errors surface at the next visit or auditErrors surface the day they're recorded
The board gets your assuranceThe board gets your judgement on evidence

Observe the work, not the worker

Observers report on records and processes. CRAs see every observation about their own work first and can reply. The board never sees a CRA league table.

Authority traces to a person

Each observer's KERI identifier is delegated from Giau's. Observers are read-only, their keys are time-limited and pre-rotated, and if one misbehaves she revokes it and the record shows when.

Verify without trusting us

Observations and reviews are ACDC credentials chained to the criterion, the observer and the reviewer. An inspector checks signatures, not our word.

For presidents, CEOs and VPs

The one-minute version.

One thing: we stopped marking our own homework.

Last month our CRAs did 1,284 pieces of monitoring work on HALCYON-3. Eight AI observers checked all of it, not a sample. 1,243 items conformed and are on a signed record. 41 raised a signal, 37 were cleared with evidence, and 4 came to me. I reviewed each one, spoke to the people involved, and signed.

One mattered: a participant consented on an out-of-date form. The sampling in our monitoring plan wouldn't have caught it until next quarter. It was found in a day and corrected in a week, and the fix is a change to how we monitor, not blame for a person.

What I need from you: approve monitoring plan v2. It moves 100% of consent checks to the observers and gives our CRAs about six days a month back for participant-safety review.

1 · Coverage
100%, not a sample.

Every work item, every day, not a fraction once a quarter.

2 · Independence
The person presenting didn't do the work.

Giau observes and judges. The CRAs do. The AI checks.

3 · Proof
Every number links to a signed record.

An auditor or inspector can verify it without trusting us.
