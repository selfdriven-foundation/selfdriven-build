# TrialThread storyboard: the 48-second story for a president

> For the hero animation on [`index.html`](../../../index.html) and, later, for
> generated imagery or video (Higgsfield, ChatGPT images). Drafted 22 Oct 2026.
> All people, studies and numbers are synthetic.

---

## Who it's for and what it must do

**Audience:** the president, CEO or VP of clinical operations at a CRO, a
sponsor or a site network. They don't monitor trials themselves. They answer to
regulators, boards and investors for the trials their people run.

**The one thing they should leave with:** *We can see, and prove, that every
piece of trial work was checked, without adding people.*

**The decision we want:** approve an eight-week pilot on one study.

**What they care about** (and so what each frame must answer):

| Their question | Where the story answers it |
|---|---|
| Is this a real problem, or a process tweak? | Frames 2–3 |
| What does it cost me? | Frame 4 |
| Do I have to rip out my systems? | Frame 5 (no) |
| Can I trust AI with regulated work? | Frames 6–7 (AI checks, a named human decides) |
| What do I get, and what do you need from me? | Frame 8 |

**Arc:** trust → overload → hidden risk → consequence → **the turn** → coverage
→ judgement → proof and the ask. Four frames of problem, four of answer, so the
turn lands at the midpoint.

---

## The frames

Each frame runs about 6 seconds. The **caption** is the only on-screen text, kept
to 10 words or fewer, so it can be read at a glance in a boardroom. The
**voice-over** is for a live presenter or a later narrated video.

### Frame 1 · Trust: "Every trial runs on trust."

- **We see:** a small isometric clinic. A participant walks in. A CRA arrives
  with a laptop bag. Morning light.
- **Motion:** camera eases in; participant and CRA walk to the door.
- **Voice-over:** "Every clinical trial runs on trust: that participants are
  protected and the data is real. The person who checks that, site by site, is
  the clinical research associate."
- **Board question:** none yet. This sets the stakes.

### Frame 2 · Overload: "Eight systems. One person. Every week."

- **We see:** the CRA at a desk, surrounded by eight floating blocks labelled
  CTMS, EDC, eTMF, ISF, Safety, IxRS, Email, Excel. Tangled lines connect them.
  One document hops from block to block, and a counter ticks: *re-keyed ×5*.
- **Motion:** the blocks drift; the document hops faster and faster.
- **Voice-over:** "Today that person copies the same facts between eight
  systems, spreadsheets and email. Each copy is a chance for an error."
- **Board question:** *Is this real?* Yes, and structural.

### Frame 3 · Hidden risk: "Errors hide in the gaps."

- **We see:** a red document slips between two blocks and drops out of sight.
  A calendar flips forward six weeks. A magnifying glass finds it: *found at the
  next visit*.
- **Motion:** the slip, then the calendar riffle, then the find.
- **Voice-over:** "Checks are sampled because time is short, so problems are
  found weeks later: a consent signed on an old form, a visit out of window."
- **Board question:** *Why hasn't this been fixed?* Because the risk sits
  between systems, where no one owns it.

### Frame 4 · Consequence: "And the risk lands on your desk."

- **We see:** a boardroom table. The president's chair. Three red tokens slide
  onto the table: *inspection finding*, *delay*, *reputation*.
- **Motion:** tokens drop in one after another; the room's light cools.
- **Voice-over:** "When it surfaces, it surfaces here: in an inspection finding,
  a delayed submission, a question you have to answer."
- **Board question:** *What does it cost me?* Findings, delay and credibility.
  (Use the client's own numbers in a live pitch. Don't quote industry figures we
  can't source.)

### Frame 5 · The turn: "TrialThread: capture once. Check on arrival."

- **We see:** the tangled lines straighten into one glowing thread. The
  document enters once, at the source, and fans out to the same eight systems,
  which stay exactly where they were.
- **Motion:** the lines untangle (the visual "exhale" of the piece); a pulse
  travels down the thread.
- **Voice-over:** "TrialThread captures each record once, checks it against the
  protocol and the regulations as it arrives, and feeds the systems you already
  run. Nothing to rip out."
- **Board question:** *Do I have to replace my systems?* No.

### Frame 6 · Coverage: "Eight AI observers check 100%. Not a sample."

- **We see:** eight small observer orbs, one per Area of Focus, rise and orbit
  a stream of documents. Each document gets a green tick as it passes; one gets a
  red flag and peels off.
- **Motion:** orbs sweep with a radar pulse; ticks pop in a rhythm; one flag.
- **Voice-over:** "Eight AI observers check every piece of work, every consent
  form and every report statement, as it happens. They read; they never change
  anything. What doesn't conform is raised with its evidence."
- **Board question:** *Can AI be trusted here?* It checks; it doesn't decide.

### Frame 7 · Judgement: "Giau observes. Judges. Signs."

- **We see:** the conductor (Giau) at a calm console, one step back from the
  work, with a visible gap between the "doing" lane and the "observing" lane. The
  red flag arrives; she reviews it and touches a passkey. A blue seal stamps the
  record.
- **Motion:** the flag glides to her; a thumb press; the seal ripples outward.
- **Voice-over:** "A named person, the conductor, reviews only what the
  observers raise. She didn't do the work, so she can judge it. Her signature is
  what makes a finding final."
- **Board question:** *Who is accountable?* A person, with a verifiable signature.

### Frame 8 · Proof and the ask: "Proof on one page. Start with one study."

- **We see:** back in the boardroom, now warm. One signed page on the table with
  three lights: *participants protected*, *data trustworthy*, *on track*. A
  small badge: *100% observed · signed*. Then the line: *Approve an eight-week pilot.*
- **Motion:** the page slides across to the president's seat; lights come on
  one by one.
- **Voice-over:** "Every month you get three answers on one page, every number
  linked to a signed record an inspector can verify. We'd like to prove it on one
  study, in eight weeks."
- **Board question:** *What do you need from me?* One study, eight weeks.

## Visual language

- **Style now:** simple isometric 3D, built from flat-shaded blocks drawn in
  code (SVG). Warm neutral ground, ember accent `#DE5A31` for the thread and the
  product, blue `#2E6F9E` for human judgement, green for conforming, red for a
  finding. Faceless, friendly figures; no logos; no real brands on screen.
- **Rule of colour:** ember is TrialThread, blue is a human decision, red is
  risk. Never use ember for risk.
- **Continuity:** the same eight system blocks appear in frames 2, 3 and 5; the
  same boardroom in frames 4 and 8 (cold, then warm). Those repeats are the story.
