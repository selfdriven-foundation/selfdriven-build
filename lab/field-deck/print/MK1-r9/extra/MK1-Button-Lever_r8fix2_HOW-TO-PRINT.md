# Button lever: how to print (2026-10-08)

One file: `MK1-Button-Lever_r8fix2_PRINT-THIS.stl`. It holds three levers side by side, lying flat. Print it exactly as it
comes; do not turn the parts. About 3 g for all three.

**PETG, not PLA.** The lever is a spring. PLA is brittle, and over time it stays bent.

Settings are the same as for the test corner, except **support OFF**:
PETG-HF, 0.2 mm layers, 4 wall loops, 20 % sparse infill, X-Y hole compensation 0, elephant foot compensation 0.15,
outer brim 5 mm, support off.

Why three: each one cools while the nozzle is on the others, so the thin parts come out cleaner. The other two are spares.

**In the slicer you should see:**

- no support anywhere;
- each lever lying on one large flat face;
- the brims of the three joining into one.

If the slicer shows any warning, don't print yet. Send Claude a screenshot first.

**After printing:**

1. Leave the plate to cool, then take the levers off and remove the brim.
2. Look at the rounded tip, the end that points into the case. If it has a small bump or a string, scrape it off. That tip is what presses the button, so it must be smooth.
3. Tell Claude. Fitting a lever to the test corner on the deck comes next, one step at a time.

This lever is r8fix2. An independent check asked for one change before printing: the side of the tip toward the case
bottom is 0.5 mm wider, inside the case only.
