From zero to earning

Four steps to become a verifier.

01
Get your activation code
Complete the short Expression of Interest form. Once your obligations are confirmed and you agree, you're emailed a unique operator licence code. Open the form on selfdriven.tel →
02
Install the Unity app
Grab it (Android or iOS) via the install links on selfdriven.tel. Use your everyday phone, or repurpose an old one sitting in a drawer.
03
Run the app
Select "Get Started" → "Licence Operator", enter your code, then sign in with email. Make sure it stays running with notifications on — that's all there is to it.
04
Get rewarded
Receive your share of network revenue as UP tokens (1 UP = 1 USD). Claim to a crypto wallet, or apply for a spending card. Optional tasks can earn you more.
Worth knowing: at this stage of the Unity Network rollout, activation credits aren't required. Later, maintaining the app is expected to cost around US$2/month (TBC), which would reduce your verification rewards accordingly. Installing the app shows you the current terms before you commit — think of it as running a nano-business.
